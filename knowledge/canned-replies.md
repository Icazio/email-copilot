## Photo Enhancer AI 与 PhotoPro AI 的区别
I'd be happy to clarify the differences between AVCLabs Photo Enhancer AI and AVCLabs PhotoPro AI.

AVCLabs Photo Enhancer AI
- Image enhancement: improve quality, reduce noise, adjust colors and tones.
- AI colorization: automatically colorize black-and-white photos.
- Image upscaling: increase resolution up to 40x.
- Background removal: automatically remove backgrounds.
- Batch processing: supports processing multiple images at once.

AVCLabs PhotoPro AI
- Object removal: remove unwanted elements like people, wires, or text.
- AI image enhancement and upscaling.
- Other features: matting, stylizing, colorizing, replacement, inpainting.
- Note: PhotoPro AI does not support batch processing.

Summary recommendation:
- If your main needs are image enhancement, upscaling, background removal, and batch processing, Photo Enhancer AI is the most suitable.
- If you require advanced editing features such as object removal and stylizing, PhotoPro AI is recommended.
