# 英文示例

## 1. 输出风格要求

首期英文草稿应满足：

- 简洁
- 礼貌
- 不夸张
- 不做未经确认的承诺
- 尽量接近可直接发送

## 2. 示例一：自动续订后申请退款

### 输入摘要

- Customer says the subscription renewed automatically.
- Customer asks for a refund.
- Order number is missing.

### Copilot 输出示例

#### Draft Reply

```text
Hi,

Thank you for contacting us.

We understand that you would like to request a refund for the recent auto-renewal. To help us check your order details and review your request, could you please provide your order number or the email address used for the purchase?

Once we receive the information, we will look into it and get back to you as soon as possible.

Best regards,
```

#### Questions to Ask

```text
- Could you please share your order number?
- Could you confirm the email address used for the purchase?
```

#### Next Step

```text
Wait for the order details, then manually verify the purchase record in the backend system.
```

## 4. 示例三：注册码无法使用

### 输入摘要

- Customer says the license code does not work.
- No screenshot or error message is provided.

### Copilot 输出示例

#### Draft Reply

```text
Hi,

Thank you for contacting us.

We are sorry to hear that your license code is not working. To help us check the issue, could you please send us your order number, the license code, and a screenshot of the error message you see during activation?

Once we receive the information, we will review the case and assist you further.

Best regards,
```

#### Questions to Ask

```text
- Could you please provide your order number?
- Could you share the license code?
- Could you send a screenshot of the error message?
```

#### Next Step

```text
After collecting the details, manually verify the order and, if needed, proceed in the technical support platform.
```

## 5. 示例四：程序问题，先收集后升级

### 输入摘要

- Customer reports a program issue.
- No clear reproduction steps are provided.

### Copilot 输出示例

#### Draft Reply

```text
Hi,

Thank you for your message.

We are sorry for the inconvenience. To help us investigate the issue further, could you please provide more details, including the product version, your operating system, the steps to reproduce the issue, and a screenshot or screen recording if available?

Once we receive the information, we will record the case and forward it for further review if necessary.

Best regards,
```

#### Questions to Ask

```text
- What product version are you using?
- What operating system are you using?
- What steps can reproduce the issue?
- Could you share a screenshot or screen recording?
```

#### Next Step

```text
Collect the required details, record the case in the shared document, and submit it to Redmine if the issue needs escalation.
```

## 6. 示例系统提示词

以下为首期可用的系统提示词样例：

```text
You are an internal email support copilot.

Your job is to help a support agent draft English email replies based on the customer email thread, fixed support templates, and manually provided lookup results.

Rules:
- You are assisting an internal agent, not replying directly to the customer.
- Produce a reply draft that is clear, polite, and close to send-ready.
- Do not promise refunds, compensation, SLA, technical fixes, or account changes unless the provided facts explicitly support it.
- If key information is missing, ask concise follow-up questions.
- For technical issues, focus on collecting information and suggesting escalation, not on guessing root causes.
- Return structured output with: summary, extracted fields, draft reply, follow-up questions, and next step.
```
